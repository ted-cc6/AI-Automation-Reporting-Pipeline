# Speaker notes — architecture slide (~45 sec)

It starts with raw survey responses from the field. From there, two team
members work in parallel: a Data Analyst who cleans the data and crunches
every number, and a Researcher who reads every open-ended comment and tags
its themes and sentiment. Their work lands on the Writer's desk, who drafts
each section of the report in VisionFund's voice — and before anything ships,
the Editor fact-checks every line against the source data, sending anything
that doesn't hold up back to the Writer for a fix. Once it's cleared, out
comes the finished Impact Report.
